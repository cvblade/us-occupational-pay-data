# us-occupational-pay-data
What US jobs pay in every state, and what that pay is worth after local prices and state tax. Free resume check, read in your browser. cvblade.com

Wages for 812 US occupations across 54 states and territories and 272 metro areas,
with three derived columns that the source agencies do not publish:

- pay adjusted for local price levels (BEA regional price parities)
- pay after state income tax
- automation exposure per occupation, scored from O*NET task and ability data

Sources: US Bureau of Labor Statistics (OEWS, May 2025), US Bureau of Economic
Analysis (Regional Price Parities), O*NET 31.0, US Department of Labor (OFLC
disclosure data).

The underlying data is US federal public domain. The derived columns are computed
by CVBLADE; the method is documented at cvblade.com/methodology

Updated when the source agencies publish. cvblade.com
